# Loopa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

## Overview

Loopa is a music creation app that runs entirely on your device. We take your privacy seriously and have designed Loopa to work without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal data.

## Data Collection

**Loopa does not collect any personal data.** Specifically:

- No account registration or sign-in is required
- No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted
- No analytics or tracking is implemented
- No advertising SDKs are included
- No crash reporting services send data off-device

## Data Storage

All data created in Loopa (sessions, tracks, recordings, settings) is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage directory. This data:

- Never leaves your device automatically
- Is not backed up to cloud services by the app
- Is only shared when you explicitly choose to export (e.g., sharing an audio file or .loopa project file)

## Microphone Access

Loopa requests microphone permission for the vocal recording feature only. Microphone audio is:

- Processed and recorded locally on your device
- Never transmitted to any server
- Only saved when you explicitly record a vocal track

## Third-Party Services

Loopa does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, ad networks, or cloud storage providers.

## Data Deletion

Since no data is collected or stored remotely, there is no remote data to delete. Local session data can be deleted at any time by:

1. Deleting individual sessions within the app
2. Using the "Clear All" function
3. Uninstalling the app (removes all app data)

## Children's Privacy

Loopa does not collect data from users of any age, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

## Contact
