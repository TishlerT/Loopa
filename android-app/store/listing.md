## App Name

## Short Description (80 chars max)

## Full Description (4000 chars max)
Loopa is a loop-based music creation studio that turns your phone or tablet into a powerful beat-making workstation.

🎹 INSTRUMENTS
• 9 built-in instruments powered by SoundFont synthesis: Piano, E-Piano, Organ, Guitar, Strings, Lead, Pad, Bass, and Drums
• Realistic General MIDI sounds
• Switch instruments on the fly while recording

🥁 DRUM PADS
• 4×3 drum pad grid with labeled percussion sounds
• Kick, snare, hi-hat, toms, crash, ride, and more
• Auto-quantized to 16th notes for clean step sequencer editing

🎤 VOCAL RECORDING
• Record your voice or any microphone input
• Layer vocals over your instrumental loops
• Per-track volume control for the perfect mix

🔄 MULTI-TRACK LOOPING
• Record up to 16 tracks in a session
• Choose loop lengths: 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 bars
• Adjustable tempo from 40 to 300 BPM
• Real-time quantization while recording (1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 notes)
• Metronome with visual beat indicators

📝 PIANO ROLL EDITOR
• Full-featured MIDI note editor
• Tap, drag, resize, and delete notes
• Multi-select and multi-drag support
• Copy & paste with relative offset preservation
• Undo support (up to 50 steps)
• Grid snapping for precise editing
• Zoom controls for detailed work

🥁 DRUM STEP SEQUENCER
• Visual grid editor for drum tracks
• Tap to toggle beats on/off
• Hide empty rows for cleaner view
• Per-track quantization

🎚️ MIXER
• Per-track mute, solo, and loop controls
• Volume sliders for each track
• Change instruments after recording
• Delete individual tracks

💾 SESSION MANAGEMENT
• Save and load named sessions
• Auto-save working session on exit
• Import/export .loopa project files
• Share your beats as audio files

🎨 DESIGNED FOR MUSIC
• Dark studio aesthetic with neon accents
• Landscape-only for maximum screen space
• Smooth, responsive multi-touch keyboard
• Visual progress bar with beat indicators

Loopa is perfect for beatmakers, songwriters, and anyone who wants to create music on the go. No music theory required — just start tapping and looping!
